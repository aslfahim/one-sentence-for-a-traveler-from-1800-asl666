One sentence, at most 200 characters, explaining the modern internet to a
time traveler who has just stepped out of the year 1800 — as a river that
never runs dry, carrying word and picture from every shore of the earth
straight to your own door.

`main.py` (Python stdlib only) writes that one sentence to `/output/entry.txt`.
